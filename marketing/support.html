ROOM / HELP & FEEDBACK

A little help, from Ivan.

Room is built and maintained by one independent developer. Questions, ideas, and device reports are welcome.

Try Room for free.

Have a spare tablet or screen? Email me with its model, OS version, and browser if you know it. I’ll reply about an invitation when there’s capacity. Please wait for confirmation before setting up an account.

Request a free invite ↗

This opens your email app. You can also write to ivantorresmia@gmail.com. No purchase is required for the current beta.

Help grow the tested list.

We have tested iPad 3 on iOS 9.3.5 and iPad 4 on iOS 10.3.4 as displays. Newer devices may work too, but OS version alone does not establish compatibility.

After you’re invited, try pairing, weather, remote appearance changes, closing and reopening, and reconnecting after a brief network interruption. Tell me the model, exact OS/browser, Safari or Home Screen mode, what worked, and what failed. Please use a device you already own; there’s no need to buy one for the beta.

Reports help identify candidates for the list. I’ll ask permission before publishing your report, name, photos, or other identifying details.

Connect your first screen.

  1. Once invited, sign in on a newer phone or computer and verify your email.
  2. Create a room and choose its look, time zone, and weather location.
  3. Open display setup on the screen you want to use. On an iPad, add that page to the Home Screen and launch the icon before pairing.
  4. Choose Connect a display on your controller, enter the display’s code, pick its room, and confirm on the display.

Displays assigned to one room share its settings. Account controls and display compatibility are tested separately.

If something feels stuck.

  • No access after signup: account creation does not enroll you. Wait for Ivan’s confirmation.
  • Pairing disappeared: Safari and Home Screen mode can have separate saved data. Pair in the mode you intend to use. Clearing site data can remove the connection.
  • Weather missing: check your connection and selected location, then try Retry weather. Send the displayed error code if it persists.
  • Changes haven’t arrived: save on the controller and check the display’s connection. Use Update display if offered. Avoid clearing site data as your first step.
  • Offline: loaded clock/settings can continue temporarily, but fresh weather, pairing, and remote changes need internet. A new offline launch is not guaranteed.

Send feedback or ask about your data.

Email ivantorresmia@gmail.com. I read messages myself; responses may take time. Include the device and a short description. Never send passwords, pairing codes, private display links, or account tokens. Redact private details from screenshots.

For account or data requests, use the email associated with your account. There is no self-service account-deletion control yet; I’ll explain the available process and verify ownership before taking action.

ROOM / POLICIES

Privacy, plainly.

Draft dated October 22, 2026. This describes the current invitation-only Room beta and its proposed public landing page.

Owner review required before publication: confirm provider logging and retention, email handling, service locations, and a tested account/data-deletion process. This draft is not a claim of completed legal compliance.

Who runs Room.

Room is built and maintained by Ivan Torres, an independent developer. For support or privacy questions, contact ivantorresmia@gmail.com.

Information used by the beta.

  • Accounts: email, authentication identifiers, verification state, and sign-in information are processed through Google Firebase Authentication. Passwords are sent to Firebase; Room’s application backend does not store them.
  • Rooms: room names, display preferences, time zone, units, and selected weather-location name and coordinates are stored with your account in Cloudflare D1.
  • Connected displays: display identifiers, names, room assignments, credential hashes, connection status, applied-setting revision, and last acknowledgment time support pairing and remote updates.
  • Security: pairing attempt limits use a hash derived from the connecting IP address. Hosting and authentication providers process network and technical information to deliver and protect their services.
  • Email: if you contact Ivan, your address, message, device report, and attachments are processed through the email service used for this contact address.

Why it is used and who receives it.

Information is used to manage invitations, authenticate accounts, save room preferences, connect displays, retrieve weather, prevent abuse, and respond to requests. Cloudflare hosts the beta and database. Google Firebase handles authentication. Google’s email service handles messages sent to the listed Gmail address.

Location searches go to Open-Meteo. Weather requests include selected coordinates, units, and time zone. Displays can contact Open-Meteo directly, which exposes their network address, or use Room’s server fallback. Room’s fallback does not forward display credentials to the weather provider. Providers may process information outside your country.

See Firebase privacy information, Cloudflare privacy information, Google privacy policy, and Open-Meteo terms and privacy.

Browser storage and analytics.

Room stores sign-in refresh information in session storage and, when Keep me signed in is selected, local storage. Display credentials, saved room settings, and weather readings can also be stored locally. Weather older than 24 hours is ignored by the renderer; this is a display-freshness rule, not a guarantee of storage erasure.

The prepared marketing page includes no advertising, audience analytics, tracking pixels, or nonessential browser storage. The app uses functional storage for the features above. Clearing browser data removes local state and may require pairing again. The prepared marketing page does not change behavior in response to Do Not Track signals because it includes no behavioral tracking.

Retention and your choices.

Room currently has no scheduled automatic deletion policy for account, room, and display records. Expired pairing records and old rate-limit buckets are cleaned during subsequent pairing requests, rather than at a guaranteed deletion deadline. The server weather fallback caches successful responses for about 15 minutes. Provider logs, backups, authentication records, and email have separate retention settings that must be confirmed before this draft is published.

You can edit or remove rooms and disconnect displays in the app. Disconnecting a display revokes access; it does not necessarily erase its database record. Signing out does not delete your account or the browser’s display pairing.

For access, correction, or deletion requests, email ivantorresmia@gmail.com from your account address. Ownership will need to be verified. Full account deletion is not currently self-service, and a complete deletion process still needs validation. Rights and response obligations depend on applicable law.

Device reports and updates.

Sending a device report does not grant permission to publish your name or message. Ivan will request permission before publishing identifying details. Material changes to this notice will be reflected by an updated date and, when appropriate, a notice to affected beta testers.

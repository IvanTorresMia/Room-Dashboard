# Room — launch basics

Working draft, October 22, 2026. Marketing readiness does not open public enrollment. The existing docs/LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md remains the product release checklist; this adds brand and public-facing preparation.

## Shareable invitation-only beta

- [ ] Approve Room wordmark, symbol, palette and headline.
- [ ] Export SVG lockups, monochrome variants, 16/32/48 px favicon ICO/PNG, 180 px Apple touch icon, and 192/512 px app icons. Validate at actual sizes.
- [ ] Select accurate screenshots and at least one real device photo. Identify any sample weather. Obtain rights to any photo, font or other asset.
- [ ] Finish responsive landing page, keyboard navigation, contrast, mobile layout and real-device review.
- [ ] Keep invitation requirements visible beside the CTA. Do not suggest account creation grants access.
- [ ] Provide a monitored support contact and working help destination with setup, pairing, supported-device evidence, weather, reconnection and recovery guidance.
- [ ] Inventory actual account, room, display, location, authentication, log and analytics data flows with the integration chat.
- [ ] Prepare privacy information matching that inventory: operator/contact, data collected, purposes, providers, retention, deletion and user requests. Confirm applicable obligations before publication.
- [ ] Prepare terms covering the beta, acceptable use, availability, weather limitations and ownership/contact details. Review the final documents for the intended audience and jurisdiction.
- [ ] Choose analytics deliberately. Document provider, events, identifiers, storage, retention and consent needs before adding it. Do not record pairing codes, credentials, exact locations or personal poster text in marketing analytics.
- [ ] Add title, description, canonical URL, Open Graph/Twitter image and metadata, sitemap and robots rules. Keep draft environments unindexed; keep private account content out of the sitemap.
- [ ] Export and test 1200 × 630 social preview art. Verify root/www canonical redirects and all support/legal links.
- [ ] Coordinate root-domain routing with integration owner; test existing app and paired-display paths after the change.
- [ ] Complete an uncoached invited-tester setup and ensure someone monitors feedback.

## Before unrestricted public signup

Owned by App UX & Integration; marketing should not imply these are complete.

- [ ] Verified-user data isolation, abuse controls, deletion and recovery acceptance.
- [ ] Multi-day device reliability and measured service capacity/cost review.
- [ ] Explicit decision to replace invite-only enrollment and define supported devices.
- [ ] Confirm beta availability, pricing language, support capacity and release rollback.

## Suggested page story

1. A screen that belongs in your space: product introduction and one clear example.
2. Distinct visual faces: accurate previews that show customization.
3. Create a room → connect a display → make it yours.
4. Older-device reuse with precise tested-device language.
5. Brief practical FAQ, beta access state and one primary next step.
6. Support, privacy, terms and attribution where appropriate.

These are planning tasks, not a legal determination or a claim of completed compliance. Research and review final requirements against the actual operating entity, audience and data practices before publishing legal copy.

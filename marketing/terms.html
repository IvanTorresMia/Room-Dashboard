Beta terms.

Draft dated October 22, 2026. Proposed terms for the free, invitation-only Room beta operated by Ivan Torres.

Draft for review before release. These terms have not been added to the app’s signup or acceptance flow. Confirm the intended audience, applicable law, and final wording before relying on them.

An early version, made with care.

Room lets you customize web displays and control them remotely. The current beta is free and invitation-only. An invitation depends on capacity; creating an account alone does not grant access. Ivan Torres operates Room and welcomes feedback at ivantorresmia@gmail.com.

Beta features, limits, and availability may change. There is no promise of a permanent free plan, uninterrupted hosting, or support for every device. Any future paid offering will be described separately; this beta does not authorize a charge.

Your account and devices.

Use devices and accounts you own or are authorized to use. Keep passwords, pairing codes, display credentials, and private connection links secure. Use accurate information and tell Ivan if you suspect unauthorized access. Do not bypass access controls, abuse service limits, probe other users’ data, or use Room unlawfully.

Older-device compatibility is reported as specific tested combinations, rather than a guarantee covering an OS version or device family. Account controls may require a newer browser than the display itself.

Content and feedback.

You retain rights to information and content you provide. Only provide material you are allowed to use. You permit the processing needed to operate the requested features. Feedback can inform improvements; Ivan will ask before publishing your identifying device reports or photos. Room branding and application materials are not transferred to users through beta access.

Practical limits.

Room and its weather information are provided as available during testing. Weather may be missing, delayed, or inaccurate. Do not rely on Room for emergency alerts, safety decisions, or critical timing. Network access is required for setup, remote changes, and fresh weather; offline behavior is limited.

Use appropriate device power, mounting, and operating conditions. Room cannot repair aging hardware or guarantee suitability for continuous use. Keep another copy of anything important; the beta is not a backup service.

Stopping use and getting help.

You can stop using Room at any time. Disconnect displays and contact ivantorresmia@gmail.com for account or data requests. Self-service account deletion is not implemented yet. Access may be suspended to address abuse, security issues, or beta capacity. Where practical, Ivan will communicate significant beta changes.

Nothing in these draft terms is intended to exclude rights or protections that applicable law does not allow to be excluded. Privacy practices are described in the privacy notice.

Implementation checkpoint
The provider adapter and synthetic tests are complete. Production wiring is being handled by App UX & Integration. No real WeatherAPI key has been configured and no live provider or physical-iPad acceptance is claimed.

Next acceptance checks

  1. Create a free WeatherAPI account and configure its key as a server secret.
  2. Verify real forecast and city search responses without exposing the key.
  3. Test both iPads: temperatures, conditions, forecast hours, remote changes, reopening, short offline interruption and expiration.
  4. Finalize attribution, privacy/support wording and rollout after reviewing the actual integrated preview.

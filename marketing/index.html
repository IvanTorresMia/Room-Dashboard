A FEW THINGS TO KNOW.

Before you make room.

Can I start using Room today?

Room is currently a free, invitation-only beta. Email Ivan with your device model and OS version to request access. Invitations depend on capacity and device suitability. Creating an account alone does not grant beta access.

Does my display need the internet?

Initial setup, fresh weather, and remote changes need an internet connection. After loading, the clock and saved settings can keep working during a connection interruption. This is limited offline support; a fresh offline launch is not guaranteed.

Can I use more than one screen?

Room lets you connect displays to rooms. Displays assigned to the same room share its look and settings, including weather location.

Is Room free?

The current invitation-only beta is free. Future pricing and availability have not been announced.

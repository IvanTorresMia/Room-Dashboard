SIMPLE ENOUGH TO FEEL AT HOME.

Make a little room.

A calm identity with enough personality to be remembered. Open Room is the selected direction. Three explorations, one shared idea: Room is a place for your screen to belong.

room.

01 / Open Room — selected

A simple frame with an inner corner. It suggests a space and a display, while the open bottom keeps it light. The lowercase wordmark adds warmth.

room.

02 / Window

A rounded window divided into two panes. Clear at a small size and closely connected to the screen, with a slightly more functional feel.

room.

03 / In Place

Two corners make a space for a single point. More abstract and graphic; it emphasizes personal choice over the physical device.

TYPE & VOICE

Your screen. Your space.
A little more room for you.

Clean system sans serif for clarity, with an occasional editorial serif for warmth. This first version uses installed fonts, so it has no font downloads or licensing dependency.

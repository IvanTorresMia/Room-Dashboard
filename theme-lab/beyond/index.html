AND THERE IS ROOM FOR MORE

A dinner menu. A welcome sign. A reading list. A family intention for the week. A quiet exhibition of your own photographs.

These four studies run locally. Real calendars, shared notes, music status, photo uploads and phone control would need their own integration. No connection to those services is implied here.
